The playground registers three families, not one

A picker with one entry demonstrates nothing, and the three states an
application has to handle are exactly what an inventory decides: a family whose
faces are all designed, one missing some of them, and one only the device has.

Inter stays complete. Source Serif 4 has no bold italic, so a bold run inside
an italic one is leaned from the bold. JetBrains Mono ships only its regular,
so both bold and italic are drawn from it. Georgia and Courier New are
`systemCandidates` — they draw on screen and an export resolves past them.

The added faces come from `@expo-google-fonts`, which publishes the complete
Google Fonts TTFs rather than the per-script subsets `@fontsource` does. A
subset would have reintroduced exactly the gap the typography guide warns
about, one family down.
