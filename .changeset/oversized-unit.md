A table row, or another block that cannot split, that is taller than a page starts a page of its own, as Word starts it, instead of being drawn over the rest of the page it was met on. It still does not split, so what does not fit on its page is not shown. What follows such a block, or a line taller than a page, starts the next page: no blank pages are left after it.
